# BridgeTranslate: first user journey

This document defines the crossing before any hosting platform or programming framework is chosen.

## Purpose

BridgeTranslate is a universal communication bridge.

It is particularly useful when natural, nonlinear, neurodivergent, disabled, dyslexic, hyperlexic, alexithymic or multilingual communication meets a linear system such as the NHS, benefits, local government, housing, employment, education or formal complaints.

The user remains the owner of the meaning. The bridge helps map, check and format it.

---

## Screen 1: Welcome

### Main message

> Say it your way. Add anything that helps explain it. BridgeTranslate helps turn your meaning into a form another person or system can understand.

### Supporting message

- You do not need a diagnosis.
- You do not need to organise your thoughts first.
- You may use a Context / About Me document to explain communication barriers or access needs.
- You decide whether the bridge understood you correctly before any final document is produced.

### Actions

- **Start crossing**
- **How this works**
- **Privacy and AI processing**
- **Language help**

---

## Screen 2: Access and consent

### Invitation access

- Enter invitation code.
- No account creation.
- No password to remember.

### Consent summary

The user confirms that:

- AI will process the text and files they choose to submit;
- they are responsible for removing unnecessary names, addresses, reference numbers and other identifying information;
- BridgeTranslate is not legal, medical or benefits advice;
- they will review the result before using it;
- the active session can be erased when finished.

### Actions

- **I understand, continue**
- **Go back**

## Screen 3: What would help the bridge understand you?

Diagnosis is optional. Users choose communication patterns using large icon-led cards.

Suggested cards:

- 🕸️ **My thoughts arrive connected**  
  I may explain several linked things before the main point becomes visible.

- 🔎 **I know what I mean but cannot find the word**  
  I may describe people, objects or roles by function or relationship.

- 🌀 **My information comes out in a different order**  
  Please organise it without removing useful context.

- 📚 **I understand complex ideas but forms confuse me**  
  Institutional questions may not match how I organise meaning.

- 🌫️ **Feelings or body signals are hard to identify**  
  I may know something is wrong before I can label the impact.

- ⏳ **I need time before the full explanation arrives**  
  Important context may appear later.

- 🌍 **English is not my first language**  
  Use clear English and avoid unnecessary idioms.

- ❓ **I am not sure**  
  Help me work out what kind of translation support I need.

Optional separate choices:

- Autism
- ADHD
- AuDHD
- Dyslexia
- Hyperlexia
- Alexithymia
- Other / exploring / undiagnosed

These labels must never be required to use the bridge.

### Actions

- **Continue**
- **Skip this step**

## Screen 4: Add context and evidence

Two clearly separated upload areas prevent context from being mistaken for evidence.

### A. Context / About Me

Optional material about:

- communication style;
- disability or access needs;
- relevant background;
- recurring barriers;
- preferred language or terminology.

This material helps interpretation. It is not automatically treated as proof of a specific event.

### B. Documents about what happened

The user may add whatever is relevant, including:

- letters;
- forms;
- emails;
- screenshots;
- reports;
- notes;
- photographs of paperwork;
- existing timelines.

No arbitrary product-level file limit is defined here. Each implementation must test real user material and explain any unavoidable technical boundary honestly.

### Actions

- **Add Context / About Me**
- **Add documents or screenshots**
- **Continue without files**

---

## Screen 5: Say it your way

### Main prompt

> Tell the bridge what happened, what you are trying to explain, or what you need help saying.

### Guidance

> Fragments, repetition, tangents, emotion, voice notes and unfinished thoughts are allowed. Do not organise it first.

### Inputs

- large text box;
- speech input where supported;
- paste existing notes;
- optional question: **What outcome do you need?**

### Actions

- **Map what I mean**
- **Back to documents**

---

## Screen 6: Meaning check

The bridge presents a plain-language map before drafting anything formal.

### Suggested structure

- **What happened**
- **Why it matters**
- **What you want the other person or organisation to understand**
- **What outcome you appear to be asking for**
- **Important dates, people or evidence found**
- **Anything uncertain or missing**

### Main question

> Is this what you are trying to say or describe?

### Actions

- **Yes, that is my meaning**
- **Not quite, let me edit it**
- **Ask me a question**
- **Go back and add more**

The bridge must not proceed to a polished final document until the user confirms or edits this meaning map.

## Screen 7: Choose where the meaning needs to go

Large icon-led destination cards:

- 🩺 **NHS, GP or healthcare**
- 🧾 **PIP, benefits or form answer**
- 🏛️ **Council or public service**
- 🏠 **Housing**
- 💼 **Employer or reasonable adjustments**
- 📣 **Complaint or escalation**
- 🗓️ **Timeline and evidence map**
- 💬 **Message to a friend, relative or colleague**
- 📧 **Email or letter**
- 🌐 **Instructions for a larger webpage or AI project**
- 🧭 **I am not sure, suggest the best format**

The user may choose more than one output where the same confirmed meaning needs different forms.

---

## Screen 8: Draft check

The bridge produces the chosen format and asks:

> How is this?

The result should be editable on screen.

### Actions

- **Change the tone**
- **Make it shorter**
- **Add more detail**
- **Keep more of my own voice**
- **Make it more formal**
- **Show what came from which document**
- **Edit it myself**
- **Yes, you bridged the gap**

The system must preserve the confirmed meaning and must not invent dates, facts, diagnoses, legal duties or evidence.

---

## Screen 9: Take the result

Available outputs depend on the job, not on a fixed hierarchy.

Possible outputs:

- editable Word document;
- PDF;
- plain text;
- structured timeline;
- evidence index;
- message or email;
- form response;
- complaint draft;
- prompt/instructions to continue a larger project in another AI workspace.

### Actions

- **Download Word**
- **Download PDF**
- **Copy text**
- **Download timeline or evidence pack**
- **Start another output from the same confirmed meaning**
- **Erase this session**

## Non-negotiable rules

1. The user owns the meaning.
2. Diagnosis is never required.
3. The bridge accepts natural input before asking for linear structure.
4. Context and evidence remain distinguishable.
5. The bridge reflects meaning back before drafting.
6. Uncertainty is shown, not guessed away.
7. The final result remains editable.
8. No permanent account history is required for the peer version.
9. The reference specification remains platform-neutral.
10. Organisations adopting the bridge remain responsible for their own hosting, security, retention and legal obligations.
