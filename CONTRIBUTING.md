# Contributing

BridgeTranslate is a public-benefit project with limited maintainer capacity. Issues and pull requests may be reviewed slowly, and no response time is promised.

Before contributing:

1. Read [ORIGIN.md](ORIGIN.md) and preserve the user's ownership of meaning.
2. Do not include personal evidence, confidential material, credentials or identifying test data.
3. Keep facts, user account, evidence, conflict, inference and uncertainty in separate lanes.
4. Mark significant design changes clearly.
5. Test accessibility and the meaning-confirmation crossing, not only visual presentation.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit it and agree that it may be distributed under the licence applying to the part of the repository you change.

A contribution does not grant authority to speak for Marie Meronym or to describe a fork as the official BridgeTranslate service.
