# Public-benefit and free-core commitment

BridgeTranslate exists to reduce the translation burden placed on people whose natural communication does not match a system's expected language or structure.

## What stays free

Published BridgeTranslate source releases and the reusable specification are intended to remain available for anyone to study, use, adapt and integrate under their published open licences.

Licences already granted for a published release cannot be withdrawn from people who follow their terms. The project intends future core releases to remain openly available too.

## What people may charge for

People and organisations may charge for hosting, compute, support, training, integration, customisation or specialist professional services. They may not remove the obligations of the applicable licence, misrepresent the origin of the work, or imply endorsement.

## What free does not promise

This commitment does not promise:

- a permanently free hosted service;
- free AI-provider or infrastructure usage;
- uninterrupted maintenance or support;
- suitability for legal, medical, clinical, benefits or other high-stakes use.

A deployer is responsible for security, privacy, accessibility testing, model choice, retention rules, lawful processing and human review in its own environment.

## Governing principle

The user owns the meaning. A system adopting BridgeTranslate must not silently turn communication differences into lost agency, invented certainty or weaker evidence.
